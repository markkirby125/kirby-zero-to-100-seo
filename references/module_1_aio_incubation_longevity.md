# Module 1: AIO Parasite Incubation & The 5-Year Longevity Playbook

*Source: Charles Floate (The Edward Show E1193 + Zero-to-100 Ep 1136 lineage). Execution mechanics of the platforms themselves live in `../../kirby-saas-syndicate/SKILL.md` Module 1 — this module defines when and why a zero-authority launch uses them.*

### **1.1 The Incubation Gap**
A new domain faces two structural delays: the fresh-site sandbox for organic rankings and the 24–72h AI Overview quarantine (§3.4). Parasite platforms are reported to bypass both: third-party authority plus UGC signals are said to let commercial pages rank within a day, and AI Overview citation is said to favor high-authority platforms for commercial queries even while the parasite URL itself sees ordinary rank volatility (all reported, single-source, unverified; a thin commercial page on a host platform can still be classed as abuse, so seed only assets with standalone value, `../../kirby-saas-syndicate/SKILL.md` Module 1 §1.1). **Incubation means capturing the query territory on rented authority while the owned domain's authority matures underneath it.**

### **1.2 AIO Parasite Incubation Strategy**
1.  **Query mapping:** Take the Stage 2 topical map's money queries. For each, note whether an AI Overview / AI Mode citation currently exists.
2.  **Platform seeding:** For the top 3–5 head queries, seed one parasite asset per query (selection matrix and 5-step production pipeline: `../../kirby-saas-syndicate/SKILL.md` Module 1 §1.3–§1.4). Comparison format first: a single syndicated comparison article is reported to be able to shift an AI Overview for its head term, and 85% of AI brand citations for commercial queries are reported to originate from third-party domains (reported, single-source, unverified). Every such asset follows the byline and common-ownership rules of `../../kirby-saas-syndicate/SKILL.md` Module 1 §1.6.
3.  **Brand-name anchoring:** Every parasite asset names the brand and links the bridge funnel (parasite → owned-domain comparison/trust page). The owned domain must be the conversion destination from day one, even while it cannot rank.
4.  **Association lock:** Parasite assets reinforce the §1.1 primary association only — incubation that sends mixed category signals poisons the 10x-reversal economics.
5.  **Measurement:** Track AIO citation presence for the target queries weekly. Success = brand cited (via parasite or directly) in AI Overviews for head queries before the owned domain exits its first quarter.

### **1.3 The 5-Year Parasite Longevity Playbook**
Parasite placements decay through rank volatility, platform policy shifts, and competitor DMCA attacks. Long-lived operations run three maintenance loops:
*   **301 redirect spine:** Expired-domain 301s pointed at a parasite URL (not the owned domain) are reported to keep its link equity replenished (single-source, unverified). Redirect only to a profile or property the user controls, and only where its first screen carries a visible handover disclosure (who owns it now, and since when); otherwise do not redirect. Domains are vetted for relevant history before redirect (Wayback check per the domain staging doctrine in `../../kirby-saas-syndicate/SKILL.md` Stage 2; handover rule in its dispatcher Stage 3).
*   **Tier-2 maintenance:** A small recurring tier-2 budget for moderated placements only, pointed at the 301 layer and at the parasite's internal-link donors (arbitrage SOP: `../../kirby-saas-syndicate/SKILL.md` Module 1 §1.5 step 2), refreshed quarterly. No mass non-editorial tier-2 blasts, and any paid link carries the sponsored disclosure of saas-syndicate Module 1 §1.3.
*   **Multi-platform redundancy:** Every load-bearing commercial query is covered by at least two parasite properties on different platforms, so a single takedown or platform canonical change does not erase the citation footprint (DMCA defense protocol: `../../kirby-off-page-seo/SKILL.md` Module 21).

### **1.4 Handover to the Owned Domain**
When the root domain hits the Stage 4 baseline (10k monthly organic), reverse the bridge direction: owned-domain comparison/trust pages become the canonical destination, parasite assets link to them as citations rather than funnels, and the §1.3 maintenance budget shifts toward the owned domain's link profile. Parasite assets that no longer map to a money query are retired (410, per `../../kirby-seo-deployment/SKILL.md` §7.7 doctrine) rather than left to decay into policy risk.

### **1.5 Guardrails**
*   Explicit user confirmation is REQUIRED before executing any parasite tactic (this skill's Stage 4 guardrail applies to this module). In non-interactive or `-y` mode, draft only: never publish a parasite asset, a redirect or a paid placement (`../../kirby-saas-syndicate/SKILL.md`, dispatcher Stage 3).
*   Every placement must pass the SRA boundary test (`../../kirby-aiseo-skill/SKILL.md` Module 15 §15.3). Unmoderated authority-rental on legacy media is struck doctrine — incubation uses moderated UGC/dev platforms only.
